Puppy Patterns

The Shelter Genetics Case

The shelter is preparing for its biggest adoption event of the season. But one puppy family is already drawing questions.

Meg, the adoption coordinator, has opened a new case file — and your name is on it.

In this lesson, students investigate observable patterns across a puppy family, evaluate which evidence is relevant to inheritance, and construct and communicate an explanation for why offspring resemble their parents without being identical to them.

Learning Objectives

1Obtain and organize evidence about similarities and differences between parents and offspring.
2Evaluate which observations support explanations about inherited traits.
3Construct and communicate an evidence-based explanation of how genetic information passes from parents to offspring while producing variation.

Standards & Practices

📐 Arizona Science Standard

5.L3U1.9 — Students obtain, evaluate, and communicate information about patterns between the offspring of plants and animals, including humans, and construct an explanation of how genetic information is passed from one generation to the next.

🔬 Science & Engineering Practices

Obtain, Evaluate, and Communicate Information · Construct Explanations · Analyze and Interpret Data · Engage in Argument from Evidence

🔗 Crosscutting Concept

Patterns

⭐ Stories in Science Framework Alignment

Students enter an authentic role as Junior Genetics Investigators, choose distinct evidence pathways, combine individual puppy data at convergence points, construct and revise an evidence-based explanation, test it with plant and human evidence, and generate an exportable professional shelter display and complete case journal.
